bed.mp3: the film's bed, 20 s at 120 beats a minute in C (C, A minor, F, G): an easy groove, lighter on the poster and fuller from the programme on, a drop under the call to register at 15.6 s.
It was made for this OpenMotionStudio template, synthesised from sine, saw and noise waves (no samples or recordings
of anyone else's), and levelled to -16 LUFS.
