kinetic.mp3: the film's score, 20 s at 120 beats a minute in A minor, its hits on the picture's: a drop and a hit under each letter of SHOUT at 4 s, the groove under the weight wave, a hit a word from 10 s, the pad alone at 14 s, a last drop at 16 s.
It was made for this OpenMotionStudio template, synthesised from sine, saw and noise waves (no samples or recordings
of anyone else's), and levelled to -16 LUFS.

Source: https://github.com/motionhubdev/openmotionstudio (apps/desktop/resources/templates/)
