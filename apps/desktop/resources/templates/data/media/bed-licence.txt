bed.mp3: the film's bed, 20 s in A minor: a pad that changes chord with each scene, a soft pulse and a low bass, a bell on each scene's first frame and on the projection's peak.
It was made for this OpenMotionStudio template, synthesised from sine, saw and noise waves (no samples or recordings
of anyone else's), and levelled to -16 LUFS.

Source: https://github.com/motionhubdev/openmotionstudio (apps/desktop/resources/templates/)
