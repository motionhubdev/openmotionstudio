beat.mp3: a four-bar loop at 120 beats a minute in A minor (A minor, F, C, G), 8 seconds and a short tail, a beat
every 0.5 s from 0. It was made for OpenMotionStudio's "Cut to the beat" template, synthesised from sine, saw and noise
waves (no samples or recordings of anyone else's), and levelled to -16 LUFS.

Source: https://github.com/motionhubdev/openmotionstudio (apps/desktop/resources/templates/music/)
