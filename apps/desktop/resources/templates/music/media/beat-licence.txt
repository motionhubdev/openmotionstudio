beat.mp3: a 20-second track at 120 beats a minute in A minor (A minor, F, C, G), a beat every 0.5 s from 0, in five
parts of two bars: an intro, a build with a riser, a drop, a breakdown on the pad alone, and an outro ending on a chord
at 18 s. It was made for OpenMotionStudio's "Cut to the beat" template, synthesised from sine, saw and noise waves
(no samples or recordings of anyone else's), and levelled to -16 LUFS.

Source: https://github.com/motionhubdev/openmotionstudio (apps/desktop/resources/templates/music/)
